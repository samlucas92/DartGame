# Career finance assumptions

The early-career economy is benchmarked against current official UK figures, then scaled to a single amateur player rather than copied directly from whole-household data.

- ONS median full-time gross weekly earnings, April 2025: **£766.60**.
- ONS median part-time gross weekly earnings, April 2025: **£280.00**.
- ONS average UK household expenditure, FYE 2025: **£676.60 per week**.
- ONS median household disposable income, FYE 2025: **£39,200 per year**.
- ONS average UK private rent, January 2026: **£1,367 per month**; a one-bedroom property averaged **£1,109**.
- England average Band D council tax, 2025–26: **£2,280 per year**; average per dwelling was **£1,770**.

Game values deliberately represent a lower-cost fictional district and shared accommodation at the start. A working amateur accrues £613/week for 32 hours and receives £2,452 on the four-week monthly payday cycle. The default shared-flat, local-travel, club-board and essentials bundle costs £380/week. Moving home or buying transport changes recurring costs. These values are gameplay abstractions, but their scale now reflects the official benchmarks above.
